# Email AI Tells - Before & After Examples

## How to Spot AI in Emails

This document shows real examples of AI-drafted emails vs. emails that sound like Tony wrote them.

---

## Example 1: Status Update Email

### ❌ **BEFORE (Obviously AI)**

```
Subject: Q2 Financial Performance Summary

Dear Executive Leadership,

I trust this message finds you well. I am writing to apprise you of our Q2 
financial performance results. Upon comprehensive review, it appears that we 
have achieved notable success across multiple key performance indicators.

The following metrics warrant your attention:

• Revenue Performance: We have successfully exceeded our quarterly revenue targets 
  by achieving $2.8M in sales, representing 112% of our projected quarterly goal. 
  This achievement reflects strong market demand and effective sales execution.

• Operational Efficiency: Our operational metrics have demonstrated substantial 
  improvement, with efficiency gains reaching 18% compared to the previous quarter. 
  This improvement is attributable to process optimization and resource allocation 
  improvements across our organization.

• Customer Retention: Our customer retention rate has remained stable at 98.2%, 
  which represents a consistent level of customer satisfaction and loyalty.

I would welcome the opportunity to schedule a meeting at your earliest convenience 
to discuss these results in greater detail. Please do not hesitate to contact me 
should you require any additional information or clarification regarding this matter.

Respectfully yours,
Anthony Chiu
```

**AI Tells Present:**
- "Dear Executive Leadership" - generic
- "I trust this message finds you well" - formulaic template
- "Upon comprehensive review, it appears" - overly formal
- "apprise you" - AI-formal (nobody says this naturally)
- Every bullet point is too long and formal
- "I would welcome the opportunity to schedule a meeting" - weak CTA
- "Please do not hesitate to contact me" - classic AI phrase
- "Respectfully yours, Anthony" - too formal for Tony
- No contractions
- No personality
- Reads like a corporate template

---

### ✅ **AFTER (Tony's Voice)**

```
Subject: Q2 Results - Revenue Strong, Churn Worth Watching

Hi [Team],

Here's the snapshot: We beat revenue targets by $300K (112% of plan), and 
efficiency is up 18%. But I want to flag one thing: customer churn moved the 
wrong direction.

The wins:
• Revenue: $2.8M (beat forecast)
• Efficiency: +18% (strong execution from ops)
• Retention: 98.2% (holding steady vs. last quarter)

The concern:
Churn increased 1.5 percentage points. It's not a crisis yet, but if this 
trend continues for 2 more quarters, we'll have a real problem. I want to 
understand the root cause before we see it compounding.

I'm attaching the full financial deck. Let's sync Wednesday to dig into the 
churn data and talk through potential responses.

Tony

---
Tony Chiu
[Title]
```

**Why This Works:**
- Subject line is specific (revenue strong, churn issue)
- "Hi [Team]" - natural and professional
- Opens with direct summary ("Here's the snapshot")
- Uses contractions naturally ("I'm", "we'll")
- Acknowledges wins clearly
- Flags concern without overstating
- Specific CTA ("Let's sync Wednesday")
- Signature is minimal
- Reads like Tony actually wrote it
- Clear perspective (this is analysis from someone who owns the outcome)

## Example 2: Problem Alert / Urgent Issue

### ❌ **BEFORE (AI Formal, Wrong Tone for Crisis)**

```
Subject: Infrastructure Incident Notification and Escalation

Team,

An infrastructure incident of significant severity has occurred that necessitates 
immediate escalation and coordinated response across multiple departments. The 
production environment is currently experiencing performance degradation that may 
potentially impact customer-facing systems and result in suboptimal user experience.

It is imperative that all department leads convene immediately to establish a 
crisis response protocol and mitigate the associated risks. The following 
information is provided to assist with your response coordination:

Status Information:
• The database system is experiencing elevated response times
• Current performance metrics indicate a 10x increase in query latency
• Customer-facing features including checkout and order status pages are affected
• The root cause has not yet been definitively determined

Required Actions:
• Database team: Please provide comprehensive diagnostics and recovery timeline
• Operations team: Please identify any recent infrastructure changes or deployments
• Quality Assurance team: Please monitor system performance and customer impact metrics

I would be grateful if each department could provide their status updates at the 
earliest opportunity so we can coordinate a comprehensive recovery effort.

Thank you for your immediate attention to this critical matter.

Best regards,
Tony
```

**AI Tells:**
- Way too formal for an urgent crisis ("necessitates immediate escalation")
- Overly long introduction (should be direct)
- "It is imperative" - AI corporate speak
- "would be grateful if" - weak (not urgent enough)
- Treating urgent crisis like formal memo
- No sense of urgency in tone
- Punctuation/structure looks like formal report

---

### ✅ **AFTER (Tony's Voice - URGENT)**

```
Subject: PRODUCTION: Database Response Time Spiked - Action Needed

Team,

We have a production issue right now. Database query times are up 10x over 
the last hour and it's affecting customer-facing features.

What we know:
• Issue started 2:47 PM ET
• Affecting checkout and order status pages
• DB CPU at 95% (normally 40-50%)
• Root cause unknown—diagnostics in progress

What I need RIGHT NOW:
• Database team: Get on the bridge call. Need full diagnostics ASAP.
• Ops team: Check for any recent deployments or configuration changes.
• QA team: Monitor customer error rates on the dashboard in real-time.

I'm in the bridge call now. If you have critical updates, call my cell.

Tony
```

**Why This Works:**
- Subject line immediately signals urgency (caps on PRODUCTION)
- Direct statement of problem (no preamble)
- Concise sections (What we know / What I need)
- Action-oriented language ("RIGHT NOW", "ASAP")
- Specific instructions (who, what, when)
- Indicates Tony is actively engaged
- No formality that would slow response
- Reads like actual crisis communication

## Example 3: Technical Explanation Email

### ❌ **BEFORE (Jargon Salad)**

```
Subject: Infrastructure Modernization Architecture Update

Team,

I wanted to apprise you of our strategic initiative regarding infrastructure 
modernization and architectural optimization. We are leveraging microservices 
architecture patterns to enable horizontal scalability across distributed 
infrastructure. This approach facilitates containerized deployment paradigms 
with reduced operational overhead and enhanced system resilience through 
compartmentalization of failure domains.

The implementation of this architecture will necessitate organizational 
restructuring and process re-engineering across development and operations 
teams. Additionally, we will need to establish new monitoring and observability 
protocols to ensure system reliability across the distributed topology.

Key considerations for stakeholder alignment:
• Enhanced operational complexity due to distributed system management requirements
• Implementation timeline estimated at Q3-Q4 with phased rollout strategy
• DevOps capability augmentation requirements for infrastructure management
• Organizational change management protocols to facilitate team adaptation

I would welcome the opportunity to schedule a meeting to discuss the viability 
and resource implications of this initiative.

Best regards,
Tony
```

**AI Tells:**
- Pure jargon ("microservices architecture patterns", "containerized deployment paradigms")
- Explains nothing ("compartmentalization of failure domains" - what does that mean??)
- Overly formal ("I wanted to apprise you")
- No business context ("Why are we doing this?")
- "would welcome the opportunity to schedule a meeting" - weak
- Reads like tech doc, not email from Tony

---

### ✅ **AFTER (Tony's Voice - Technical but Clear)**

```
Subject: System Architecture Shift - Moving to Microservices

Team,

Here's what we're doing: Instead of one big monolithic system, we're moving 
to smaller independent services that can scale separately. Why? Because if 
one part breaks, the whole system doesn't go down. Plus, teams can ship 
faster without waiting for others.

The benefit:
• Failure isolation (one service breaks, others keep running)
• Independent scaling (handle spikes without scaling everything)
• Faster deployments (each team ships their service independently)

The cost:
• More operational complexity (more services to monitor = more tooling)
• Team restructuring needed (we need stronger DevOps capability)
• Timeline: Q3-Q4 (phased rollout, not a flag day)

Questions we need to answer:
• Do we have the DevOps capacity to support this complexity?
• What's the full implementation timeline with dependencies?
• Who owns the integration layer between services?
• What monitoring/alerting needs to be built?

Let's grab 30 min this week. I want to pressure-test this against actual 
operational risk before we commit.

Tony
```

**Why This Works:**
- Explains technical concept in plain language
- Connects to business benefit ("teams can ship faster")
- Acknowledges tradeoffs honestly
- Specific resource questions (not vague)
- Suggests next step (30 min meeting, not "let me know")
- Tony's skeptical approach ("pressure-test this")
- No jargon, but technically sound
- Reader understands what's being proposed without PhD

## Example 4: Decision Request Email

### ❌ **BEFORE (AI Verbose)**

```
Subject: Strategic Initiative - Market Expansion Opportunity

Dear [Recipient],

I hope you are well. I am writing to propose that we discuss a potential 
strategic initiative that has come to my attention through market analysis 
and competitive positioning research. Upon reflection, it appears that current 
market conditions may present an opportune window for us to consider geographic 
diversification or vertical market penetration into adjacent customer segments.

Specifically, I have identified the healthcare vertical as a potentially viable 
expansion opportunity. The healthcare market demonstrates characteristics that 
align favorably with our current product capabilities and market strengths. 
Further analysis suggests that healthcare institutions are actively migrating 
away from legacy systems, which aligns with our core competency.

However, I must note that expansion into the healthcare market would necessitate 
compliance with HIPAA regulations, which would require additional implementation 
effort and organizational capability building.

I would respectfully suggest that we schedule a meeting at your earliest 
convenience to discuss the viability of this opportunity and determine whether 
it represents a strategic priority for our organization going forward.

Respectfully yours,
Tony
```

**AI Tells:**
- Overly long preamble
- "I hope you are well" / "Upon reflection, it appears" - templates
- Hedging language ("potentially viable", "may present")
- "I would respectfully suggest" - weak
- No clear question or decision point
- Missing key details (market size, timeline, resource needs)

---

### ✅ **AFTER (Tony's Voice - Clear Decision Request)**

```
Subject: Market Opportunity - Healthcare Vertical

[Recipient],

I'm looking at healthcare as a potential new market for us. Before I spend 
a lot more time on this, I want your take on whether it fits our strategy.

Why it caught my attention:
• Hospitals are actively migrating from legacy systems (our strength)
• Market is 3x larger than our current segments
• Competitors haven't moved aggressively into healthcare yet (timing window)

The reality check:
• HIPAA compliance adds ~6 months implementation overhead
• We'd need to build new security/audit capabilities
• Resource investment: 2-3 people dedicated for Q3/Q4

My thinking: If we move now, we could have a certified offering by Q4 and 
start selling into healthcare in Q1. But it requires prioritizing this over 
other initiatives.

Questions for you:
1. Does healthcare align with our long-term strategy?
2. Can we absorb the compliance/resource investment?
3. Should we greenlight this or pass?

Let's grab 30 min this week. I want your perspective before I go deeper.

Tony
```

**Why This Works:**
- Direct opening (no preamble)
- Clear structure (Why / Reality / Thinking)
- Specific details (timeline, team effort)
- Acknowledges constraints and tradeoffs
- Specific questions (not vague)
- Explicit decision request ("should we greenlight this?")
- Clear next step (30 min meeting)
- Tony's analytical but decisive tone

## Example 5: Feedback / Correction Email

### ❌ **BEFORE (AI Corporate Softening)**

```
Subject: Project Deliverable Review and Feedback

Hi [Team],

I wanted to take a moment to acknowledge the efforts that have been invested 
in the recent project deliverable. I have reviewed the documentation and wanted 
to provide some constructive feedback that may be beneficial for future iterations.

While there are certainly many positive aspects of the work that has been 
produced, there appear to be some areas that may benefit from additional 
refinement. Specifically:

• The timeline projections may not fully account for potential technical 
  dependencies and integration complexities.
• The resource allocation model may warrant reconsideration in light of 
  historical velocity data.
• The risk assessment section appears to underestimate certain variables 
  that have historically impacted similar projects.

I would suggest that we consider a revised approach that incorporates these 
observations. I remain available to discuss these items at your convenience 
and would welcome the opportunity to provide additional clarification.

Best regards,
Tony
```

**AI Tells:**
- Overly polite ("I wanted to take a moment to acknowledge")
- Weak language ("may benefit", "appear to", "may warrant")
- Sugar-coating criticism with "positive aspects"
- Vague recommendations ("consider a revised approach")
- "I remain available" - passive

---

### ✅ **AFTER (Tony's Voice - Direct but Fair)**

```
Subject: Project Plan - Need Revisions Before We Proceed

[Team],

I reviewed the plan. Good foundation, but there are 3 gaps we need to fix 
before we approve this.

Issues:
1. Timeline doesn't account for vendor dependencies. You're assuming they 
   deliver in parallel—history says they delay. Add 3 weeks buffer.

2. Resource estimate is light. Based on our velocity data from similar projects, 
   add 40% more engineering time. You're underestimating the integration work.

3. Risk section is too optimistic. You listed some risks but ranked them too 
   low. The platform dependency is a real risk if they change their API—needs 
   a mitigation plan, not just acknowledgment.

Redo these three sections and send back. I want to see:
• Revised timeline with vendor delay buffer
• Updated resource estimate (show your math)
• Risk mitigation strategies for top 3 risks

Send by Thursday so we can review Friday and move forward next week.

Tony
```

**Why This Works:**
- Direct statement of issue ("gaps we need to fix")
- Specific problems with examples
- Clear why ("history says they delay")
- Actionable next steps (what to redo, specific deadline)
- Shows Tony is engaged with details
- Fair but firm (not emotional, just factual)
- Fast execution (review Friday, move forward Monday)
- Tone: respectful but no nonsense

## Quick Comparison Table

| Element | AI Style | Tony's Style |
|---------|----------|-------------|
| **Opening** | "Dear Leadership Team" | "Hi [Name]," |
| **Greeting close** | "I trust this finds you well" | (Skip or "Quick update:") |
| **Main point** | "I am writing to inform you..." | "Here's what's happening:" |
| **Structure** | Perfect bullets, uniform length | Varied paragraphs, mixed lengths |
| **Emphasis** | ***Everything emphasized*** | **Key terms only** |
| **Contractions** | "I am", "we are", "do not" | "I'm", "we're", "don't" |
| **Formality** | "It is my understanding" | "I'm seeing" |
| **Call-to-Action** | "Please let me know" | "Let's sync Wednesday" (specific) |
| **Urgency** | "At your earliest convenience" | "By EOD Thursday" (specific deadline) |
| **Closing** | "Best regards, Anthony" | "Tony" |
| **Tone** | Corporate formal | Professional + direct + personable |

## Validation Checklist for Emails

Before sending any email drafted with AI help:

- [ ] Opening and closing match tone? (both formal OR both casual)
- [ ] Contractions used naturally? (I'm, we're, don't - not "I am")
- [ ] Paragraphs vary in length? (1 sent. to 5 sent., not uniform)
- [ ] No generic AI phrases? (not "feel free to reach out", "best regards")
- [ ] CTA is specific? (includes action + deadline, not "let me know")
- [ ] Tone matches recipient? (peer/manager/executive level)
- [ ] Subject line is clear? (specific topic/issue, not clickbait)
- [ ] Bullets strategic? (not just breaking up every sentence)
- [ ] Emphasis purposeful? (only **key terms**, not ***everything***)
- [ ] Reads naturally? (feels like Tony wrote it without AI help)
- [ ] No jargon salad? (technical explained clearly if needed)
- [ ] Length appropriate? (1-3 min read, or schedule meeting instead)
- [ ] No excessive formality? (unless speaking to external board)

## Remember

**Good AI-assisted email:**
- Shows Tony's thinking/analysis
- Gives enough context to act
- Has specific next steps
- Maintains Tony's voice throughout
- Looks like Tony wrote it in 15 minutes

**Bad AI-assisted email:**
- Generic corporate template
- Overly formal
- Vague next steps
- Reads like a bot wrote it
- Would make Tony cringe reading it

**Test:** Would Tony send this? If not, it needs rewrites.
