Listing filters

        

          

            Qualities to list
            
            
            
            
          

          
          

            Pricing fallback
            
            If a Shadow/Gold/Diamond card has no active listings, derive a floor from the Meteorite's recent-sale median (or floor) × 5 / 25 / 125 (forge cost). Lets the bot list shines that nobody else is selling yet.
          

          

            Recent-sales guard
            
            
            When the last N sales averaged BELOW the current lowest ask, list at the sales-avg price instead of undercutting the (too-high) ask. Prevents your listing from sitting at a price nobody's actually paying.
          

          

            Aggressive undercut
            
            
            
            Chases the market's lowest ask no matter how low it goes, EXCEPT it won't undercut below the safety floor. Useful when you want to stay cheapest on illiquid cards but avoid listing at dump-priced levels.

Quick start

      
    
        
  1. Open the Wallet tab. Paste in a burner wallet address and its private key. Save.
    2. 
        
  2. Open the Settings tab. Tweak the parameters if you want — the defaults are sensible. Save.
    3. 
        
  3. Open the Run tab. Click Start bot. The log shows what's happening live.
    4. 
        
  4. Leave the window open. The bot loops on the cadence you set in Settings.
    5. 
      


      
What the parameters mean

      
    
        
  • Relist threshold — how much the market has to move before the bot bothers re-pricing your listing. Higher = fewer fees, looser tracking.
    • 
        
  • Max undercut relists per cycle — when you keep getting undercut by other sellers, this caps how many cards the bot will chase per cycle. Protects against IMX-fee burn during price wars.
    • 
        
  • Dump-protection ratio — if the cheapest market listing is wildly below the recent sale price, the bot refuses to list (someone is dumping; don't follow them down). 90% is a safe default.
    • 
        
  • Full cycle interval — how often the bot scans every allowed set. Default is once per day.
    • 
        
  • Fast cycle interval — for the "fast sets" (usually the newest set, where prices move fast). Default 30 min.
    • 
        
  • Qualities — which shines the bot will list. Meteorite is safe; Shadow/Gold/Diamond are higher-value but illiquid — dump protection matters more for these.
    • 
      


      
If something goes wrong

      
    
        
  • Click Open bot.log on the Run tab to see the full log.
    • 
        
  • Click Open bot folder to inspect or back up config.json, .env, and the token cache.
    • 
        
  • If the bot won't start, you probably haven't filled in the Wallet tab yet.
    • 
        
  • Crashes from RPC errors are auto-recovered. If you see "exit code 1" you'll likely also see a clear error in the log.
    • 
      


      
What this bot does, in one sentence

      
It lists every card in your wallet (that matches the filters) on the Immutable zkEVM marketplace at 1 wei below the cheapest competitor, with safeguards against dumping and undercut wars, and re-prices whenever the floor moves.
